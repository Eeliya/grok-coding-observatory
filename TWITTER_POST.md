## Best Practices

### Posting Tips:
1. **Timing:** Post Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM EST
2. **Pin it:** Pin to your profile for a few days
3. **Engage:** Reply to every comment in first 2 hours
4. **Hashtags:** Use 2-3 max (algorithm prefers fewer)
5. **Follow-up:** Post again in 1 week with updates/stats

### After Posting:
- Share in replies to relevant AI/coding threads
- Post to LinkedIn with longer explanation
- Share in Discord communities (Cursor, etc.)
- Submit to Reddit after 24 hours
